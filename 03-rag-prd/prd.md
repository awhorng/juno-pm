## Problem & user

RocketShip PMs need evidence-based prioritization. Juno turns noisy Slack, tickets, and strategy docs into a ranked, cited backlog they can defend.

## Solution overview

**Retrieval strategy:** Hybrid

Juno should review a group of issues to identify patterns.

## Retrieval requirements (RAG)

- **Sources:** Juno must access the issue tickets that customers submit. Ideally, tickets from the last three days.
- **Chunking / indexing:** Hybrid (Semantic + Keyword)
- **Grounding rule:** We need the AI to verify whether this issue is a defect or a bug.
- **Freshness:** The data should refresh every 15 minutes to keep the queue up to date, but it isn't refreshing and is bogging down resources.

## Requirements

| # | Requirement | Priority | Acceptance criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Retrieval quality and latency | Must | The logs should look at the top 5 issues to verify an issue. |
| 2 | Fail-safe on empty retrieval | Must | Juno should defer to a Product Manager to investigate. |
| 3 | Grounded trust | Must | We need the AI to verify whether this issue is a defect or a bug. |

## Out of scope

Decisions that cannot be cited to a source in the knowledge base. Anything Juno is not allowed to retrieve or act on without a human.
